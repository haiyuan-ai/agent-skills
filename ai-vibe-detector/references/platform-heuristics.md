# Platform Heuristics

These are practical heuristics that may correlate with detector risk on some platforms.

They are weak signals. Use them carefully.

## Possible Weak Signals

- excessive transition markers
- highly regular paragraph length
- repeated summary-style endings
- polished but generic wording
- many abstract nouns with few concrete referents
- overuse of explanatory parentheticals

## Important Caveats

- These signals are genre-sensitive.
- A technical guide or formal announcement may naturally trigger several of them.
- Presence of a weak signal does not imply AI authorship.

## How to Use in Reviews

Say:
- “这类表达可能提高被看作模板化写作的风险”
- “这是弱信号，需要结合全文结构和文本类型判断”

Do not say:
- “出现这个就说明是 AI”
- “改掉这一点就一定能过检测”
